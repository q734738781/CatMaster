# Section organization

Use [manuscript argument](../publication-launch-writing/SKILL.md) when organizing
a paper, and shared scientific-communication guidance for reports or talks.

Choose drafting order from the available evidence and the part that needs work.
Results can be organized by scientific questions, comparisons or mechanisms;
methods explain what was actually done; discussion connects findings to the
question and competing explanations. An introduction supplies the context the
reader needs, and the abstract summarizes the supported finding and consequence.
These are purposes, not required section templates or a fixed writing sequence.
