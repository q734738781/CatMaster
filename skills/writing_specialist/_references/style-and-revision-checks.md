# Style and revision checks

For language polishing, use the relevant examples in
[scientific-communication's phrasing reference](../../writing_quality/scientific-communication/references/phrasing.md).
Preserve technical terminology, numerical meaning, citations and uncertainty.

Revise at the scope of the problem: a confusing argument may need reordered
paragraphs; a wordy sentence may need a local edit. Paragraph count, sentence
shape and the sequence of editing passes are not requirements. Reuse previous
scientific checks and resolve actual inconsistencies in quantities, labels or
citations where they occur.
