# Submission readiness

Use for an explicitly requested submission or publication-readiness task.
Follow the actual venue and study-design requirements. Ordinary report writing
does not require this process.

Inspect the manuscript's argument, scientific scope, citations and rendered
figures. Check that title, abstract and conclusion describe the supported work,
methods explain the actual study, and important counterevidence and conditions
remain visible. Repair concrete defects without requiring a fixed revision count
or a hypothetical hostile-review rehearsal.

Keep authorship, funding, conflicts and availability declarations factual. Do
not invent an author choice or repository location to complete a form. Ask about
a genuinely missing declaration when submission requires it. Keep requested
source/data files reachable and supply the venue's required deliverables.
