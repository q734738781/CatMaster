# research-evidence-reconciliation

Read the actual Result, producing Experiment, relevant hypotheses, counterevidence and sources. Separate observation, derived analysis and interpretation. Compare conditions before treating findings as contradictory; repeated analysis of one dataset is not independent confirmation.

Use `supports`, `opposes` or `inconclusive` only for the distinction actually tested. Leave unrelated hypotheses unjudged. Supply `scope` and `rationale` for the affected prediction and conditions. A failed proxy for one observable does not erase a useful relation for another observable.

When interpretation changes, retain the old H or R and connect its replacement or correction with `revise_research_claim`. `qualify` limits an earlier claim; `withdraw` records why a particular use is invalid; neither deletes the original observation. Query revisions in both directions before relying on an older claim. Do not automatically reject every downstream idea.

Determine whether the evidence warrants a new hypothesis, an existing hypothesis with narrower scope, or a correction within the same Experiment. Recommend a check only when its observation could change the judgment. Record meaningful hypotheses and experiments directly with the available graph tools. A new physical hypothesis is not required for input repair or a missing prerequisite.

For a declared stopping reconsideration:

1. Read the supplied issue and actual user scope, then inspect the strongest relevant evidence and alternatives independently.
2. Look for an overlooked action that can change the situation. Reuse a prior decision on the same issue and unchanged material evidence; a different title or agent is not new evidence.
3. If a concrete authorized validation exists, record or reuse its Experiment with inputs, a discriminating outcome and a stopping condition. Literature/recommendations authorize source reading and evidence analysis, not new scientific calculations or lab execution. External proposals remain handoffs.
4. Record the assessment with `record_research_review`, including the remedy Experiment when applicable, its authorization basis, open premises and resumption conditions. Do not execute the remedy.

Return a concise scientific explanation of what remains valid, what changed, and the recommended check or reason to park. No evidence grades, confidence scores or mandatory positive conclusions are needed. The graph is a scientific index; decisive original sources remain the evidence owner.

For literature-dependent judgments, the shared reference
`/.deepagents/skills/litreview_agent/literature-evidence-use/references/evidence-attributes.md`
describes access depth, condition fit and claim relationship. Metadata identity,
retrieval relevance and extraction confidence do not establish scientific
support. Unread metadata candidates remain unassessed. When selecting sources,
use selected, deferred or excluded with a reason, not a paper-level score.
